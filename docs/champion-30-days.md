# Champion plan: 30 days

Use this plan to introduce the spec-and-plan loop with one real story at a time. Keep the comparison practical and use your own baseline.

## Week 1: Write

Pick one real story and write the spec with your squad. Agree the acceptance criteria, constraints, non-goals and open questions before planning.

## Week 2: Plan and score

Run `/plan`, score the result with the [rubric](plan-review-rubric.md), and fix the gaps before any code is written.

## Week 3: Ship by slices

Deliver one behaviour per PR. Put the approved plan in the description and review the diff against the criteria.

## Week 4: Show and share

Compare rework and review time, then add the [spec template](specs/TEMPLATE.md) to your repo. Share one before-and-after example with the squad.

## If you only do one thing this week

Take the next story you were going to hand to Copilot, and write three acceptance criteria before you prompt.

## Measuring it

Compare planned and unplanned stories on:

- Rework, measured through follow-up commits.
- Review time.
- Copilot usage.

Do not quote numbers until you have a baseline.
